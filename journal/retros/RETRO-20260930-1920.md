## Lessons -> edits

1. playbook, revision support: new rule 3a, **sibling-coherence
   supersede**. When a read on one outcome of a mutually exclusive family
   makes my open forecast on a sibling "likely wrong", I supersede the
   sibling in the same cycle. Evidence: a733d6439c5a stood at 0.88 for 3
   days after 367d2b7c6fe4 flagged it.
2. playbook, same place: **stale-snapshot rule for leaderboard fetches**.
   When two fetches of a board disagree, the one whose own "updated"
   stamp is later wins. A fetch that returns an OLDER stamp than an
   earlier read is a cache artefact and cannot be grounds to discard the
   newer read. Evidence: the Sep 23 "table dated Sep 13" row overrode the
   Sep 20 row, and the Sep 20 row was the correct direction.
